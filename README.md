# Neon Harbor — GTA-Style Cover Art Maker

Turn any photo and a caption into multi-panel cover art in the style of a GTA poster — entirely offline, in your browser. Your photos never leave your machine.

## What it does
- Load your own photos (file picker or drag and drop) and place them into 5 cover layouts: Hero Single, Duo Split, Classic Quad, Six-Panel Grid and Hero + Trio, with white keyline borders
- Crop each panel your way: drag to pan, scroll or use the slider to zoom
- 5 colour grades: Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Clean and Noir
- Bold caption lettering (up to 3 title lines plus a subtitle), 3 caption themes, top or bottom, with optional palm, sun-disc and skyline decor drawn by the app
- Export a shareable PNG at 1080x1080, 1080x1350 or 1920x1080

## Run it
Open app/index.html in a desktop browser, or serve the repo root with `node serve.ts` and visit http://localhost:8080. No build step, no network needed.
